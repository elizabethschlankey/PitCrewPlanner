How often each volunteer has actually been assigned to help, across every event this season — a quick way to spot your most reliable regulars and anyone who's fallen off. "Reliability" is about following through on commitments, not attending every event (plenty of volunteers are only ever available for some) — it's based on signup follow-through (of events they signed up for, how many they actually helped at) and badge accountability (of badges they checked out, how many they returned), averaged when both exist. Hover a percentage for the raw numbers behind it; a volunteer with neither signup nor badge history shows "No data" instead of a score.

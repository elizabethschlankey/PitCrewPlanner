Itinerary Templates

Reusable itinerary schedules (Home Football Game, Away Football Game, UIL Contest, ...) — tag one with an event type and it becomes available whenever a matching event needs a starting timeline. Applying one only COPIES its items onto that event's own itinerary; items can still be freely added, edited, or removed afterward without touching the template.

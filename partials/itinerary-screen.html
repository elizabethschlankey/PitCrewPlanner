Paste a Schedule

Paste a schedule straight from an email or a printed itinerary — it splits into a new item at every time it finds, even if several ended up crammed onto one line (common when copying out of a PDF). "7:00 AM - Buses Depart", "7:00 Buses Depart" (24-hour), and plain lines with no time on them at all are all fine. This only adds what you paste; anything already on the list below stays untouched.
